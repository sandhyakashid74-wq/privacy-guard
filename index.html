🛡️ Privacy Guard

Understand Terms Before You Accept

Privacy Guard helps users understand important and risky Terms & Conditions before accepting them.

🔍 How Privacy Guard Works

1️⃣ Paste Terms

Copy and paste the Terms & Conditions into the text box.

2️⃣ Analyze

Privacy Guard scans the text and finds common privacy risks.

3️⃣ Understand Risks

Get a risk score, detected traps, explanations and recommendations.

⭐ Privacy Guard Features

🔍 Trap Finder

Detects common privacy-risk terms hidden in Terms & Conditions.

📊 Risk Score

Provides a simple privacy risk score from 0 to 100.

💡 Easy Explanation

Explains why a detected term may be a privacy concern.

🛡️ Privacy Awareness

Helps users understand Terms before accepting them.

ℹ️ About Privacy Guard

Privacy Guard is a web-based project designed to help users understand important privacy-related terms in Terms & Conditions.

It analyzes the text, identifies common privacy risks, provides a risk score, and explains the detected risks in simple language.

🎯 Project Objective

The main objective is to increase privacy awareness and help users make informed decisions before accepting Terms & Conditions.
